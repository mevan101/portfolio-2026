Hackathon series · May – Oct 2026 · Series lead

ML Bangladesh's first in-person hackathons, backed by MLH and Google for Developers: a free one-day format that takes students from a workshop to a deployed project. It ran at UITS, DIU, Rajshahi University, SEU, RUET, Bangladesh Agricultural University and the EMK Center, followed by Hacktoberfest editions in Dinajpur and Dhaka.
