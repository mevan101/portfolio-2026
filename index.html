01 · Selected work

Programs I've built

Competitions, hackathons and community programs, with what they produced. Most were run by volunteers and free for every participant.

  1. 01

    Build With Gemma @Bangladesh

    July 2026 · Organizer · Machine Learning Bangladesh

    ML Bangladesh's flagship hybrid hackathon at United International University, part of Google DeepMind's global Build with Gemma series. Planned in about a week, judged on Kaggle, with a prize pool funded by the Gemma team and hosted with the IEEE Computer Society UIU chapter. Six tracks covered problems such as Bangla-language access, rural healthcare, offline study tools and voice-first bookkeeping.

    May – Aug 2026 · Series lead · with MLH and Google for Developers

    ML Bangladesh's first in-person hackathons: a free, one-day campus format that takes students from a workshop to a deployed project. The series ran at UITS, DIU, Rajshahi University, SEU, RUET, Bangladesh Agricultural University and the EMK Center. Hacktoberfest Hack Days followed in Dinajpur and Dhaka in October 2026.

  3. 03

    ML Olympiad 2024: CO₂ Emissions Prediction Challenge

    Feb – May 2024 · Co-host with Shuvro Pal · TFUG North Bengal and GDG Dhaka

    A national Kaggle competition, part of Google's worldwide ML Olympiad, asking teams to predict per-capita emissions for 2030 from development indicators. It closed with an in-person finale at UIU and was featured on the Google Developers Blog.

  4. 04

    Kaggle Community Olympiad: Dhaka Breath Index

    Sep – Oct 2026 · Co-host with Shuvro Pal

    A forecasting competition on PM2.5 air pollution across Bangladeshi cities, tied to SDGs 3 and 11. Designed to reward real modelling work: submissions had to come from notebooks with documented reasoning, and AutoML or LLM starter templates were screened out.
