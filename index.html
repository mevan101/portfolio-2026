Hackathon · July 2026 · Organizer

Build With Gemma @Bangladesh

A hybrid hackathon at United International University, part of Google DeepMind's global Build with Gemma series. Planned in about a week, judged on Kaggle, with a prize pool funded by the Gemma team and hosted with the IEEE Computer Society UIU chapter. Six tracks covered problems like Bangla-language access, rural healthcare and offline study tools.

Hackathon series · May – Oct 2026 · Series lead

Build With AI Hack Days

ML Bangladesh's first in-person hackathons, backed by MLH and Google for Developers: a free one-day format that takes students from a workshop to a deployed project. It ran at UITS, DIU, Rajshahi University, SEU, RUET, Bangladesh Agricultural University and the EMK Center, followed by Hacktoberfest editions in Dinajpur and Dhaka. I also sat on the judging panel at UITS.
