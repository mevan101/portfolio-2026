2020

2021

Campus and code.

Led the AngelHack Campus Hub at my college, taught programming online with Codecademy Chapters, and was named a Crowdsource Community Champion.

2022

Into developer communities.

Joined Google Developer Group Dhaka as an Ambassador and became an Awareness360 Star Fellow.

2023

A community of our own.

Started TFUG North Bengal, today ML Bangladesh. Founded UniDevs, began computer science at UIU, and became GDG Dhaka's Communications Lead.
