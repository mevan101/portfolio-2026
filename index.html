build_with_gemma.exe

Hackathon · July 2026 · Organizer

Build With Gemma @Bangladesh

A hybrid hackathon at United International University, part of Google DeepMind's global Build with Gemma series. Delivered on a one-week planning timeline, judged on Kaggle, with a prize pool funded by the Gemma team and hosted with the IEEE Computer Society UIU chapter. Six tracks covered problems like Bangla-language access, rural healthcare and offline study tools.

build_with_ai_hack_days

Hackathon series · May – Oct 2026 · Series lead

Build With AI Hack Days

ML Bangladesh's first in-person hackathon series, supported by MLH and Google for Developers: a free one-day format that takes students from workshop to deployed project. Editions ran at UITS, DIU, Rajshahi University, SEU, RUET, Bangladesh Agricultural University and the EMK Center, followed by Hacktoberfest editions in Dinajpur and Dhaka. Kaggle competition · Feb – May 2024 · Co-host

ML Olympiad 2024: CO₂ Emissions Prediction Challenge

A national round of Google's worldwide ML Olympiad, co-hosted with GDG Dhaka, challenging participants to predict per-capita CO₂ emissions for 2030. It concluded with a two-day finale at UIU and is featured on the Google Developers Blog, which names me as host.

A forecasting competition on PM2.5 air pollution across Bangladeshi cities. Notebook-only submissions with documented reasoning filtered out unedited AutoML and LLM templates, so the rankings reflect genuine modelling work.
